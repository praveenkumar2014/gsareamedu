# Product requirements

GUIDESOFT Education & AI connects learning, skills and career development under one identity. Personas: learner discovers and enrols; instructor and mentor support learning; employer explores talent; admin operates the platform. The core slice covers discovery, enquiries, identity, catalogue publication and enrolment; other personas currently have roles but no dedicated workflows. No placement or outcome guarantees.

Success measures require owner approval and real instrumentation: enquiry-to-enrolment conversion, catalogue engagement, learner retention and support response time. Do not invent baselines. Validate privacy, pricing, program content and operational policies before launch.
